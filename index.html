Who we are

Growing minds and shaping futures

Ipusukilo Secondary School is dedicated to providing a learning environment in which learners are supported to develop academically, socially and personally. We recognise the importance of education in empowering individuals and strengthening communities.

Our school community works towards high standards of learning, good conduct, mutual respect and responsibility.
